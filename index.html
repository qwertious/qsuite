Why "no cloud"? qDocs keeps everything in this browser's own storage. qDrive moves files directly between your devices over an encrypted connection, so nothing is parked on someone else's disk. The trade-off is honest: both of your devices have to be switched on at the same time for a transfer, because there is no server in the middle holding it for later.
